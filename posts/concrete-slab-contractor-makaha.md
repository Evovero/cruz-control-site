<h2>What a Slab in Makaha Actually Needs to Hold Up</h2>
<p>Leeward Oahu soil can shift with moisture changes, which means a slab that skips proper base preparation is more likely to crack or settle unevenly over time. Before we pour anything, we grade the site, compact a gravel base, and set drainage so water moves away from the slab instead of pooling against it. Reinforcement, whether wire mesh or rebar depending on the application, gets tied in before the pour so the slab can flex slightly with ground movement instead of cracking.</p>
<p>Curing matters just as much as the pour itself. Island heat speeds up surface drying, which can lead to a weaker slab if the concrete is not kept moist during the early curing window. We manage that process rather than leaving it to chance, because a slab that cures too fast on the surface while the interior is still soft is a slab that will show problems later.</p>

<h2>Home Additions and ADU Slabs</h2>
<p>An ADU or room addition slab has to account for more than just the concrete itself. Plumbing and electrical rough-ins typically need to be set before the pour, so we coordinate timing with other trades to avoid cutting into a finished slab later. Layout also matters: door swings, hallway widths, and where fixtures will eventually sit all get planned before the forms go in, not after.</p>
<p>We treat these projects differently from a simple patio or driveway pour, since an ADU or addition slab is effectively becoming part of a livable structure. That means tighter tolerances on level and finish, and more attention to how the slab ties into the existing foundation or framing plan.</p>

<h2>Garage Floors and Shed Slabs</h2>
<p>A garage floor sees a different kind of stress than a home addition slab. Vehicle weight, regular foot traffic, and the occasional dropped tool all call for a durable finish and properly placed control joints that let the concrete contract without random cracking. We finish garage slabs with a broom or trowel texture depending on how the space will be used, and we always plan joint spacing before the pour rather than cutting them in as an afterthought.</p>
<p>Shed slabs are usually simpler in scope but still benefit from the same base preparation. A shed sitting on an unprepared pad is one of the more common causes of a sagging or cracked floor we get called out to look at, so we do not cut corners just because the footprint is smaller.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>How long does a concrete slab take to cure in Hawaii's climate?</h3>
<p>A slab typically reaches enough strength for light foot traffic within a few days, but full structural curing takes longer. We manage the curing process to account for island heat and humidity, and we let homeowners know exactly when the slab is ready for the next phase of construction.</p>

<h3>Do I need a permit for a slab addition in Makaha?</h3>
<p>Permit requirements depend on the scope of the project and whether it is attached to an existing structure. We recommend confirming requirements with the county before work begins, and we are happy to walk through what a specific project involves during a site visit.</p>

<h3>What is the difference between a slab for an ADU and a garage slab?</h3>
<p>An ADU slab generally requires coordinated plumbing and electrical rough-ins and tighter finish tolerances since it becomes part of a livable space. A garage slab is built to handle vehicle loads and regular traffic, with control joints planned around how the space will be used day to day.</p>

<h3>How thick should a concrete slab be for a home addition?</h3>
<p>Thickness depends on the intended use and the load the slab needs to carry. We assess this on site based on the specific project rather than applying a single standard to every job, since a garage floor and a bedroom addition do not carry the same requirements.</p>

<h3>Can a slab be poured during Hawaii's rainy season?</h3>
<p>Yes, with the right planning. We watch the forecast closely and schedule pours around expected rain to protect the fresh concrete, and we can adjust timing as needed to keep the project on track.</p>
